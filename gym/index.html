A look inside

What it does

  • Log a set by typing it. It saves as you type: no steppers, no confirm button.
  • Exercises sit in folders (Chest, Back, Legs, or your own), so picking one takes a couple of taps.
  • Last session's numbers appear next to every set, so you always know what to beat.
  • A 🏆 the moment you set a personal record, plus records and progress charts for every exercise.
  • A calendar with a coloured dot for each body part you trained.
  • Repeat a past workout in one tap, or share one as text.
  • kg or lb, and km, mi or m, chosen per exercise.
  • Works fully offline. Back up to a file you control, and restore it any time.

Free, and it stays that way

Every feature is free. There's an optional tip in the app if you'd like to support it. Nothing you log ever leaves your phone unless you send it. Privacy policy

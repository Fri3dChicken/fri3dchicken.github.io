I build Plain apps: small tools that do one job well and then leave you alone.

Why I'm doing this

I'm a solutions architect in Australia, and I got tired of apps that bury simple tools under ads, lock basic features behind subscriptions, and quietly collect data they don't need. So I'm building the opposite: small, honest apps that do one job well, keep your data on your phone, and cost nothing. Plain Gym is the first. I built it for my own training and use it every day.

What "Plain" means

No ads

No ad networks and no tracking code, anywhere in the app.

No paywalls

Every feature is free for everyone. If you want to say thanks, there's an optional tip.

Nothing collected

No accounts, no analytics. Your data lives on your phone, and backups go only where you send them.
